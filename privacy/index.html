PRE-ORDER INTEREST

Your information.

We ask for your name, email address and the designs you’re interested in, so Odisha Store can manage your request and contact you about it.

What a request means

You are expressing interest, with no payment, stock reservation or obligation to buy. We will confirm product details, price and availability before you decide. Your consent covers email about this request, not a general marketing subscription.

Where the information goes

When the service is open, the request is stored in the Store’s private database and viewed through its password-protected dashboard. It is not published in the catalogue. The cart saves product options on your device; your name and email are not saved in that cart. A random retry token and a token-salted request fingerprint are kept for the browser session to reduce duplicate submissions after connection failures.

Changing your mind

When we contact you, you can decline or ask us to close the request and remove your contact details. Closing a request and deleting its details are separate actions. Removing a request from the active database does not immediately remove copies from existing backups.

Before public opening

This Store is being prepared. Public collection of personal information will open only after a contact channel, retention period, private hosting and backup arrangements are in place. No customer information is needed to browse the design catalogue.
