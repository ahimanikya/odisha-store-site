COLLECTION 01 · SHARED STORY v4

One story.
Many ways to carry it.

From cups to Tasar bags—and the back of a black Odisha T-shirt.

The riverbank and sailing scenes connect the collection. ODISHA stays prominent; the T-shirt front and bottle retain the familiar emblem.

These are illustrated design concepts. Fabrics, printing and final artwork will be checked against physical samples.

Front and back concept. Not a manufactured sample.

ODS-BG-001 · Project Odisha Tasar Jhula

The story you carry.

Three ways to carry the story: two-handle designs in terracotta or sea blue, and a long natural-golden shoulder pouch. Tasar fabric and construction are proposed, pending physical samples.

The new illustrations replace the earlier emblem-only bag design. Shared flat artwork will be prepared after this direction is reviewed.

Proposed specification

Tasar silk outer shell; proposed cotton lining and reinforced handles. Body 380 x 420 mm, 80 mm gusset, 650 mm handles remain quote targets. Supplier to propose suitable fabric weight.

Tasar silk outer; terracotta and Nila Sagara blue are matching colour concepts, not final dye approvals

Illustrated front panel matching the cup stories, proposed 280 x 300 mm. Large ODISHA above the riverbank or sailing scene. Reverse undecorated for this proposal; exact print master pending.

Material and design concept. Not a manufactured sample.

A maritime story around an everyday cup. The original ivory-and-blue design sits alongside terracotta and sea-blue colour studies. Capacity, finish and care instructions await sampling.

330 ml ceramic mug; straight-sided blank preferred for the story wrap. Supplier confirms actual capacity and dimensions.

Ivory body; sea-blue handle/interior - proposed

ANOTHER WAY TO CARRY THE STORY

The long Tasar Jhula.

A relaxed pouch with one long, broad strap, natural golden Tasar and a simple button closure.

The cup’s sailing scene carries onto the front, with a prominent sea-blue ODISHA name and a fine laterite edge. More fabric stays visible around the story.

Proposed study: body about 32 × 43 cm, strap about 105–115 cm long and 5 cm wide, with lining and reinforced joins. Dimensions and carrying comfort need sample review.

This is an additional shape to compare with the two-handle bags above.
