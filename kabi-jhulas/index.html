ODIA POETRY JHULA · KABI JHULAS

For the book.
For the journey.

Twenty cloth-bag designs, each with a different border, colour or way of carrying a story.

Choose a Jhula on its own, or one of the eighteen proposed Poetry Kits. Each kit brings together the bag, a cloth notebook, refillable pen, bookmark, pen sleeve and an Odia poetry book. The two Pattachitra border studies are available as bag-only interests.

These are design studies awaiting physical samples. Materials, making techniques, poetry-book selection, prices and supply are still to be confirmed. No payment or stock reservation is made.
