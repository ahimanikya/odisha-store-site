THE THEME

A flowing floral rhythm

River Rhythm brings the movement of a flowering riverbank into a room. Large organic medallions alternate with conch-inspired curls and slender creepers, giving the pair an unfolding, asymmetrical rhythm. This is the collection’s design story, rather than a claim that every motif has one fixed traditional meaning.

The colour story

  • Ivory
  • Lac red
  • Ink black
  • Mustard

Digital colour direction; physical yarn and cloth colours remain subject to sampling.

Motifs and border placement

  • Organic floral medallions lead the ivory field; small sprigs and curls create movement between them.
  • Lac-red floral side lanes sit beside black inner lanes, with mustard accents.
  • A layered floral band, fine rails and a dark finishing band anchor the lower edge.

How the textile creates the design

Bandha is yarn-resist ikat: yarn is tied and dyed before weaving. The soft edges arise from yarn alignment. The proposed design must be developed with an ikat weaver; the digital outline is a composition guide.
