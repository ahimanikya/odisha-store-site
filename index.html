A SHARED STORY

A riverbank.
A boat setting out.

Our first collection draws on Odisha’s maritime memory: the boats we float, the journeys we imagine and the connections we carry. The illustrated voyage is an artistic interpretation, not a reconstruction of a particular ship or journey.

The collection carries the Odisha name, with shared maritime artwork across the shirts, bags, cups and bottles.
