THE THEME

Patterns of the hills. Stories of the Dongria.

Developed as a Diwali gift bag, this design balances a dense patterned front with open cloth on the back. Red, yellow and green triangles, diamonds and circular accents connect the two sides.

PROVISIONAL — Dongria motifs, caption and product use require Dongria Kondh collaboration and approval. This is a printed interpretation, not hand embroidery; community endorsement is not claimed.

Indicative digital colours. Physical cloth, yarn and printed colours await sampling.

Details that define the design

  1. Patterned front

    Stacked bands of triangles, diamonds and small circles form a tall central panel.

  2. Open back

    Small ornaments flank the Odia lettering. The water label, caption and Project Odisha logo sit above a broad lower border.

  3. Two handle options

    Natural handles give a quieter outline. Red handles and a visible zip echo the supplier bag used for the later visualization.

The coordinated story

Both versions carry the Project Odisha book-and-boat logo and the caption “Patterns of the hills. Stories of the Dongria.” The back artwork uses the supplied water wording with Odia lettering; its final spelling and letterforms need a fluent-speaker proof before printing.

Natural-coloured cloth is shown in both concepts. Fibre content, bag dimensions, handle strength, closure, printing and wash care await a physical sample. Printed stitch effects do not indicate hand embroidery.
