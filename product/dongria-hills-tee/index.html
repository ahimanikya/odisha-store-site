THE THEME

Patterns of the hills. Stories of the Dongria.

The design follows the visual rhythm of the textile photographs shared during development: rows of small triangles, larger patterned triangles, circular accents and closely spaced bands. It brings that rhythm into an everyday printed T-shirt.

PROVISIONAL — Dongria motifs, caption and product use require Dongria Kondh collaboration and approval. This is a printed interpretation, not hand embroidery; community endorsement is not claimed.

Indicative digital colours. Physical cloth, yarn and printed colours await sampling.

Details that define the design

  1. Front composition

    A compact chest panel leaves breathing room around the motif. The caption sits beneath it.

  2. Pattern rhythm

    Fine diagonal marks suggest the stitch texture in the reference cloth. Small circles interrupt the repeated triangles.

  3. Two colourways

    The same motif appears on black and white. Cream lettering suits the black shirt; burgundy lettering suits the white shirt.

The coordinated story

Both colourways carry the same caption: “Patterns of the hills. Stories of the Dongria.” Colour names describe this design palette; no fixed cultural meanings are assigned to them.

This is a printed design inspired by Kapdaganda embroidery, not a hand-embroidered garment. Fabric composition, fit, size chart, wash care and final print quality will be confirmed through sampling.
