Patterns of the Hills T-shirt

A T-shirt inspired by Kapdaganda embroidery of the Dongria Kondh community. The front pairs the Project Odisha book-and-boat logo and name with a narrow patterned band. The full pattern and “Patterns of the hills. Stories of the Dongria.” caption sit on the back. Choose black with cream lettering or white with a sea-blue name and burgundy back caption.

THE THEME

Patterns of the hills. Stories of the Dongria.

A quiet Project Odisha identity on the front pairs with the fuller textile-inspired rhythm on the back. Rows of triangles, circular accents and closely spaced bands connect the two sides.

PROVISIONAL — Dongria motifs, caption and product use require Dongria Kondh collaboration and approval. This is a printed interpretation, not hand embroidery; community endorsement is not claimed.

Details that define the design

  1. Front identity

    The book-and-boat logo and Project Odisha name sit above a narrow patterned band, with open cloth between them.

  2. Back composition

    The full patterned panel sits above the caption: Patterns of the hills. Stories of the Dongria.

  3. Two colourways

    Cream front lettering and back caption suit the black shirt. Sea-blue front lettering and a burgundy back caption suit the white shirt.

The coordinated story

The same front-and-back composition is offered in black and white. The colour names describe this design palette; no fixed cultural meanings are assigned to them.

This is a printed design inspired by Kapdaganda embroidery, not a hand-embroidered garment. Fabric composition, fit, size chart, wash care and final print quality will be confirmed through sampling.
